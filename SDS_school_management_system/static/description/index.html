01 • Overview

Everything Your School Needs in One Place

The SDS School Management System centralizes core school administration in Odoo. Staff can manage connected student, parent, teacher, academic, attendance, examination, fee and facility records while using list views, forms, dashboards and printable reports.

03 • Admission

Student Admission & Application Management

Organize the admission journey from applicant information to the internal student record. The student form can store registration, academic, personal, guardian, contact and admission details.

Admission Form

✓ Student registration information
✓ Academic year, grade and class
✓ Date of birth and personal details
✓ Parent / guardian details
✓ Contact and address information
✓ Admission date and student status

Admission Workflow

1. Capture applicant/student information
2. Review academic and guardian data
3. Confirm admission details
4. Create/activate the student record
5. Continue with fees, attendance and academics
Portal / Online Admission: If your deployment includes the Odoo Website/Portal extension, the admission process can be extended with a public application form for applicants or parents, followed by staff review and internal admission. A public portal form is an extension of the internal school-management workflow and should be enabled only when the Website/Portal components are installed.
