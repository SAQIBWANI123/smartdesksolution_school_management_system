01 • Overview

Everything Your School Needs in One Place

The SDS School Management System centralizes daily academic and administrative work inside Odoo. Staff can maintain structured student records, connect parents and teachers, organize academic structures, record attendance, manage examinations, track fees and generate school reports.

The objective is simple: keep school information connected, searchable and easy to manage while reducing repetitive manual administration.

03 • Admission & Portal

A Structured Admission Journey

Admission is the starting point of the student lifecycle. The system is designed to keep admission information organized before it becomes part of the regular student, academic and administrative workflow.

1. Application

Capture applicant/student information in a structured admission form.

2. Review

School staff can review the information before confirming the student record.

3. Enrollment

Connect the student with academic, parent/guardian and operational records.
Portal note: A public Website/Portal admission application requires the corresponding Odoo Website/Portal implementation or extension. The internal admission workflow and public portal application should be treated as separate layers.
