01 · Product overview

One operational picture for the whole school

SDS School Management System connects the records staff use every day. Start with an admission, turn an approved applicant into a student, manage academic activity, follow attendance and fees, then produce polished reports from the same Odoo database.

Students

Profiles, enrollment states, guardians, contact details, medical notes and smart statistics.

Admissions

Public applications, supporting documents, review stages and approval into enrollment.

Academic structure

Academic years, terms, grades, classes, sections and subject assignments.

Teachers

Faculty profiles, qualifications, specializations and class or subject responsibilities.

Attendance

Load a class roster, record daily status and track present or absent counts.

Examinations

Grading scales, exam results, subject marks, pass status and report cards.

Fees

Fee heads, grade structures, student statements, payments and outstanding balances.

School services

Transport, hostel allocation, library circulation and connected operational records.

02 · Portal experience

Useful to families before and after enrollment

The portal gives families a clear entry point while keeping the school team in control of review, enrollment and financial data inside Odoo.

Online admission application

Applicants submit student details, date of birth, gender, grade, academic year, guardian contacts, notes and supporting documents. Staff review applications through New, Under Review, Approved and Rejected stages.

/school/admission

Parent fee statements

Authenticated guardians see invoices, due dates, totals, paid amounts and balances for students explicitly linked to their portal account.

/my/school/fees

05 · Setup and technical details

Ready for an Odoo 19 deployment

Quick installation

  1. Copy SDS_school_management_system into a configured custom addons path.
  2. Restart Odoo and update the Apps list in developer mode.
  3. Install the module and optionally load the included demo data.
  4. Configure academic years, grades, classes, subjects and fee heads.
  5. Link portal users to guardians before enabling family fee access.

Included reports

  • Student ID Card PDF
  • Academic Report Card PDF
  • Fee Payment Receipt PDF
  • Admission documents and portal templates
  • Demo data for students, exams, fees and library records
