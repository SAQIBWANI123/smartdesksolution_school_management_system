01 · Product Overview

One operational picture for the whole school

SDS School Management System connects the records staff use every day. Start with an admission, turn an approved applicant into a student, manage academic activity, follow attendance and fees, then produce polished reports from the same Odoo database.

StudentsProfiles, enrollment, guardians, medical notes and smart statistics. AdmissionsPublic applications, documents, review stages and enrollment approval. AcademicsYears, terms, grades, classes, sections and subjects. TeachersFaculty profiles, qualifications and responsibilities.
AttendanceClass rosters, daily status and attendance counts. ExaminationsMarks, grading scales, pass status and report cards. FeesFee heads, structures, statements, payments and balances. School ServicesTransport, hostel and library operational records.
02 · Portal Experience

Useful to families before and after enrollment

The portal gives families a clear entry point while school staff keep control of review, enrollment and financial data in Odoo.

Online admission application

Applicants submit student details, grade, academic year, guardian contacts, notes and supporting documents. Staff review New, Under Review, Approved and Rejected applications.

/school/admission

Parent fee statements

Authenticated guardians see invoices, due dates, totals, paid amounts and balances for students linked to their portal account.

Setup: give the guardian a portal user, link that guardian to the student, then open the fee page after login.

/my/school/fees

05 · Setup & Technical Details

Ready for an Odoo 19 deployment

Quick installation

  1. Copy SDS_school_management_system to your custom addons path.
  2. Restart Odoo and update the Apps list.
  3. Install the module and optionally load demo data.
  4. Configure years, grades, classes, subjects and fee heads.
  5. Link portal users to guardians for family fee access.

Included reports

  • Student ID Card PDF
  • Academic Report Card PDF
  • Fee Payment Receipt PDF
  • Admission documents and portal templates
  • Demo students, exams, fees and library records

06 · Daily Use

Clear workflows for staff and families

The module keeps public applications, school operations and parent information connected while respecting each user’s access level.

Admission to enrollmentA parent submits /school/admission. Staff reviews the application, approves it, and Odoo creates the student and guardian records with their relationship connected.Attendance made simpleA teacher selects a class and the roster is generated. The teacher manually chooses Present, Absent, On Leave, Late or Excused. Absent and leave entries require a reason.
Parent information accessA portal user is linked to a guardian, and the guardian is linked to specific children. The parent can then view only those children’s fees, attendance and exam results.Backend visibilityAdministrators open Parents & Guardians inside Odoo and use the Children, Fees, Attendance and Results smart buttons to review linked records in the backend.
