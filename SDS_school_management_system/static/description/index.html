01 • Product Overview

One Connected School Workflow

The SDS School Management System brings the core activities of a school into a single Odoo environment. Instead of maintaining separate records for admissions, students, academic setup, teachers, attendance, examinations and fees, the application is designed around connected school records and operational workflows.

02 • Core Features

Complete School Administration

👨‍🎓 Students

Create and maintain student profiles, academic information, admission date, class/section, roll number, contact information, parent/guardian details and student status.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parents & Guardians

Keep guardian information connected with students so family details remain available alongside the student's academic and administrative record.

👨‍🏫 Teachers & Faculty

Manage teacher name, qualification/degree, specialization, joining date, gender, contact details, taught subjects and class responsibilities.

🏫 Academics

Configure academic years, grade levels, classes, sections and subjects with maximum marks, pass marks, subject codes and subject types.

📅 Attendance

Keep attendance information connected to student records and make attendance status available from the student workflow.

📝 Examinations

Maintain exam and marks information and turn results into structured academic report cards.

💳 School Fees

Track fee invoices, invoice dates, due dates, total amounts, paid amounts, amount due and invoice status.

🚌 Transport & Facilities

Keep transport, routes, school facilities, buildings, rooms and supported hostel-related information organized with student administration.

📚 Library

Maintain book and issue information and connect library activity with students where applicable.

04 • Admission Form & Portal Application

Complete Online Admission Form

The admission application is designed as a structured portal form for collecting a prospective student's information before the application reaches the school's internal admission team. The form can be presented as a guided, multi-step journey so applicants can enter information clearly and submit the application for review.

STEP 01

Student Details

Student name, date of birth, grade applying for and gender.
STEP 02

Guardian Details

Parent/guardian name, relationship, email address and phone number.
STEP 03

Documents

Upload supporting documents such as birth certificate, school report, photo and identity proof.
STEP 04

Review & Submit

Applicant reviews the entered information and submits the admission application.
Portal workflow: The public applicant submits the form → the school receives the application → staff reviews the submitted information/documents → the application can progress through the admission workflow → the successful applicant can then be converted into an enrolled student record.
